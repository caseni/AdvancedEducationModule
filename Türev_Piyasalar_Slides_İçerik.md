# Türev Piyasalar Eğitim İçerikleri

## 1. Türev Piyasalar Nedir?

• Derivatives Definition: Değeri başka bir finansal araç (underlying asset) değerine bağlı olan karmaşık finansal sözleşmeler; risk transfer ve price discovery araçları
• Market Structure: Exchange-traded derivatives (organize borsalar), Over-the-Counter (OTC) markets, electronic platforms, clearing houses
• Core Functions: Risk management (hedging), speculation (profit seeking), arbitrage (price efficiency), portfolio optimization, leverage provision
• Global Scale: 2025'te 640+ trilyon USD nominal value; dünya GDP'sinin 7 katı büyüklükte dev piyasa
• Economic Importance: Financial stability, price discovery, risk distribution, liquidity provision, market efficiency enhancement

**Örnek:** 🌍 2025'te CME Group günlük 3.5 milyar USD derivatives volume ile dünya liderliğini sürdürüyor.

• Forward Contracts: OTC markets'te customizable sözleşmeler; counterparty risk, tailor-made terms, private negotiations, no daily settlement
• Futures Contracts: Exchange-traded standardized forwards; central clearinghouse, daily mark-to-market, margin requirements, standardized specifications
• Pricing Theory: Forward Price = Spot Price × e^(r-q)T; r=risk-free rate, q=dividend yield, T=time to maturity
• Key Differences: Standardization (futures), credit risk (forward higher), liquidity (futures superior), customization (forward flexible)
• Practical Applications: Currency hedging, commodity price protection, interest rate management, equity index exposure, portfolio insurance

**Örnek:** 💼 Turkish Airlines, 2025'te fuel hedging ile jet yakıt maliyetlerini 180 milyon USD sabit tuttu.

## 3. Opsiyon Sözleşmeleri ve Greeks

• Options Theory: Hak veren (obligation yaratmayan) sözleşmeler; Call options (alma hakkı), Put options (satma hakkı), European vs American style
• Black-Scholes-Merton Model: C = S₀N(d₁) - Ke^(-rT)N(d₂); S₀=spot price, K=strike, r=risk-free rate, T=time, σ=volatility
• The Greeks Risk Measures: Delta (Δ) price sensitivity, Gamma (Γ) delta change rate, Theta (Θ) time decay, Vega (ν) volatility sensitivity, Rho (ρ) interest rate sensitivity
• Trading Strategies: Covered calls, protective puts, straddles, strangles, spreads (bull/bear), iron condors, butterflies
• Market Dynamics: Implied volatility surface, volatility smile/skew, pin risk at expiration, gamma squeeze effects, options flow impact

**Örnek:** 📊 2025'te Tesla opsiyon volatilitesi %45'e çıktı; gamma squeeze etkisiyle hisse %12 yükseldi.
