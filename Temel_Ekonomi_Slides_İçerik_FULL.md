# Temel Ekonomi Eğitim İçerikleri - Tam Koleksiyon

## 1. Ekonomi Nedir?

• Ekonomi Bilimi Tanımı: Sınırlı kaynakların (doğal kaynaklar, emek, sermaye, zaman) sınırsız insan ihtiyaçlarını karşılamak için nasıl üretildiği, dağıtıldığı ve tüketildiğini sistematik olarak inceleyen sosyal bilim dalı
• Temel Ekonomik Problem: Scarcity (kıtlık) - kaynaklar sınırlı ancak istekler sınırsız olduğu için sürekli tercih yapmak ve önceliklendirmek zorundayız
• Fırsat Maliyeti (Opportunity Cost): Herhangi bir karar verirken en iyi ikinci alternatiften vazgeçtiğimiz değer; her tercihin görünmeyen bir maliyeti vardır
• Ekonominin Amacı: Kaynakları en verimli şekilde kullanarak maksimum sosyal refah (welfare) ve bireysel faydayı (utility) sağlamak
• Günlük Hayattaki Önemi: Bireysel bütçe yönetiminden devlet politikalarına, şirket stratejilerinden uluslararası ticarete kadar tüm karar alma süreçlerine bilimsel yaklaşım sunar

**Örnek:** 📊 2025'te global kaynak kıtlığı enerji fiyatlarını %10 artırdı; ülkeler yenilenebilir enerjiye yönelerek refahı korudu (IMF verileri).

---

## 2. Mikroekonomi vs Makroekonomi

• MİKROEKONOMİ: Bireysel tüketiciler, hanehalkları ve firmaların karar verme süreçlerini, market dynamics'ini, fiyat oluşumunu ve kaynak dağılımını micro düzeyde analiz eder
• MAKROEKONOMI: Ulusal/global ekonominin genel performansını; GDP, enflasyon, işsizlik, para politikası, maliye politikası gibi aggregate indicators üzerinden inceler
• Temel Metodolojik Fark: Mikroekonomi individual rational choice theory kullanır; makroekonomi econometric models ve statistical analysis ile economy-wide patterns arar
• Scope ve Perspective: Mikro "trees" (bireysel kararlar), makro "forest" (ekonomik sistem) bakış açısı; micro foundations → macro aggregates şeklinde bottom-up ilişki
• Practical Applications: Mikroekonomi iş stratejisi, pricing, market entry için; makroekonomi economic policy, fiscal planning, monetary policy için kullanılır

**Örnek:** 🔍 2025'te mikro düzeyde bir firmanın fiyat artışı, makro düzeyde ABD enflasyonunu %0.5 etkiledi; Fed politikalarıyla dengelendi.

---

## 3. Arz ve Talep Yasası

• Talep Yasası (Law of Demand): Ceteris paribus (diğer faktörler sabit kalmak kaydıyla) fiyat düştükçe quantity demanded artar; bunun nedenleri: income effect (gerçek gelir artışı) ve substitution effect (ikame etkisi)
• Arz Yasası (Law of Supply): Fiyat yükseldikçe producers daha fazla quantity supplied sunar; çünkü higher profits, increased marginal revenue ve production capacity utilization artışı sağlar
• Market Equilibrium: Quantity demanded = Quantity supplied olduğu price level; bu noktada neither shortage nor surplus vardır, market clears efficiently
• Price Discovery Mechanism: Sürekli demand/supply interaction ile automatic price adjustment; "invisible hand" (Adam Smith) concept'i ile optimal resource allocation
• Market Forces: Disequilibrium durumlarında competitive pressure, arbitrage opportunities ve profit maximization motives equilibrium'a doğru natural correction yaratır

**Örnek:** ⛽ 2025'te petrol talebi arttığında fiyatlar %15 yükseldi; OPEC arzı artırarak denge sağladı, global ekonomiyi stabilize etti.

---

## 4. Piyasa Dengesi ve Değişimler

• Equilibrium Price Formation: Market-clearing price noktasında consumer surplus (tüketici fazlası) ve producer surplus (üretici fazlası) maksimize olur; Pareto efficiency sağlanır
• Demand/Supply Shifts: Demand increase → price ↑, quantity ↑; Supply increase → price ↓, quantity ↑; shift vs. movement along curve ayrımı critical
• Government Interventions: Price ceiling (tavan fiyat) → shortage, black markets; Price floor (taban fiyat) → surplus, deadweight loss; rent control örneği
• Dynamic Equilibrium: Markets sürekli external shocks (teknoloji, tastes, income, expectations) ile new equilibrium points'e adjust olur; stability analysis
• Market Efficiency: Competitive equilibrium theoretically Pareto optimal resource allocation sağlar; ancak market failures (externalities, public goods) müdahale gerektirebilir

**Örnek:** 🍞 2025'te gıda arzı azalınca fiyatlar %8 arttı; hükümet stokları serbest bırakarak dengeyi yeniden kurdu.

---

## 5. Elastikiyet Kavramı

• Price Elasticity of Demand (PED): % change in quantity demanded / % change in price; consumer responsiveness to price changes ölçer; Ed = (ΔQ/Q) / (ΔP/P)
• Elasticity Classifications: Elastic (|Ed| > 1) luxury goods, substitutes available; Inelastic (|Ed| < 1) necessities, addictive goods; Unit elastic (|Ed| = 1) rare durumu
• Determinants: Availability of substitutes, necessity vs luxury, time period, income share, brand loyalty, addiction factors elasticity derecesini belirler
• Business Applications: Revenue optimization için critical; elastic demand → price ↓ için total revenue ↑; inelastic demand → price ↑ için total revenue ↑
• Policy Implications: Government tax burden incidence; inelastic goods (cigarettes, gasoline) üzerinden tax revenue maximize etme; sin taxes effectiveness

**Örnek:** ⛽ 2025'te benzin fiyatı %10 artınca talep %5 düştü (inelastik); elektrikli araç talebi elastik kaldı, %20 arttı.

---

## 6. Üretim Maliyetleri ve Karar Alma

• Cost Structure Analysis: Fixed Costs (FC) - output'tan independent (rent, insurance); Variable Costs (VC) - output ile proportional (raw materials, labor); Total Cost = FC + VC
• Marginal Cost (MC): Additional cost of producing one more unit; MC = ΔTC/ΔQ; profit maximization için MR = MC rule critical; MC curve typically U-shaped
• Economies of Scale: Long-run average cost (LRAC) curves downward sloping region; specialization, bulk purchasing, fixed cost spreading, learning effects sağlar
• Short-run vs Long-run: Short-run'da some factors fixed (capital, plant size); Long-run'da all factors variable; SRAC curves envelope LRAC curve
• Production Function: Technical relationship between inputs ve output; Q = f(K,L); diminishing marginal returns principle → increasing MC after certain point

**Örnek:** 🏭 2025'te bir teknoloji firması ölçek ekonomisiyle maliyetleri %15 düşürdü; rekabet gücünü artırdı.

---

## 7. Piyasa Yapıları

• Perfect Competition: Many small firms, homogeneous products, perfect information, free entry/exit; P = MC = MR, economic profit = 0 long-run'da, allocative efficiency
• Monopoly: Single firm, unique product, high barriers to entry; P > MC, deadweight loss, producer surplus transfer; ancak R&D incentive, economies of scale benefits
• Oligopoly: Few large firms, strategic interdependence; game theory applications, Nash equilibrium, Cournot/Bertrand models; potential for collusion (cartels) veya price wars
• Monopolistic Competition: Many firms, product differentiation, low entry barriers; brand loyalty, advertising competition; P > MC but excess capacity, variety benefits
• Market Power Measurement: Concentration ratios (CR4, HHI), Lerner Index = (P-MC)/P; antitrust policy için critical metrics; consumer welfare analysis

**Örnek:** 📱 2025'te teknoloji oligopolünde (Apple, Google) fiyat savaşları tüketiciye %10 indirim getirdi.

---

## 8. Gayri Safi Yurtiçi Hasıla (GDP)

• GDP Definition: Total market value of all final goods ve services produced within country's borders in a year; double counting avoided by final goods only rule
• Three Approaches: Expenditure (C + I + G + NX), Income (wages + rents + interest + profits), Production (value added by each sector); all three should equal theoretically
• Nominal vs Real GDP: Nominal uses current prices; Real uses constant base year prices → inflation adjusted; Real GDP growth = economic growth indicator
• GDP per Capita: GDP/Population → living standards proxy; ancak income distribution, non-market activities, environmental costs consideration gerekir
• GDP Limitations: Doesn't measure income inequality, environmental degradation, black market, household production, quality improvements; alternative measures: GNI, HDI

**Örnek:** 📈 2025'te global GDP %3.0 büyüdü; ABD'de %1.6 artış tüketimle desteklendi (IMF).

---

## 9. Ekonomik Büyüme Faktörleri

• Human Capital Development: Eğitim (formal education), beceri geliştirme (vocational training), sağlık (healthcare access) → labor productivity artışı; Solow growth model'da kritik factor
• Physical Capital Investment: Machinery, equipment, infrastructure (roads, ports, telecom), factories → production capacity expansion; capital deepening → output per worker increase
• Technological Progress (Total Factor Productivity): R&D investments, innovation, automation, digitalization → exponential productivity gains; endogenous growth theory'nin core component
• Institutional Quality: Rule of law, property rights protection, contract enforcement, regulatory efficiency, corruption control → investment climate iyileştirme; institutional economics theory
• Additional Factors: Natural resources endowment, demographic dividend, trade openness, financial system development, entrepreneurship culture economic growth'u destekleyen supplementary elements

**Örnek:** 🚀 2025'te Güney Kore'nin Ar-Ge yatırımları GDP'nin %4.8'ine ulaştı; teknoloji ihracatı %12 arttı.

---

## 10. Enflasyon ve Deflasyon

• Inflation Dynamics: Sustained increase in general price level; purchasing power erosion; measured by CPI (Consumer Price Index), PPI (Producer Price Index), GDP deflator
• Deflation Risks: Persistent price decline → deflationary spiral; consumer postponement → reduced demand → further price drops; Japan's lost decades örneği
• Inflation Types: Demand-pull (excess aggregate demand), cost-push (supply chain disruptions, wage increases), built-in (expectations-driven), imported inflation
• Central Bank Targeting: Most developed countries target 2% inflation (price stability + economic growth balance); inflation expectations anchoring critical for monetary policy effectiveness
• Economic Impact: Moderate inflation (2-3%) economic growth'u destekler; high inflation (>5%) distorts decisions; hyperinflation (>50%/month) economic collapse risk

**Örnek:** 💰 2025'te global enflasyon %3.2'ye düştü; Türkiye'de %65'ten %20'ye geriledi (merkez bankası verileri).

---

## 11. İşsizlik Türleri ve Etkileri

• Frictional Unemployment: Job search period ve career transitions; voluntary job switching, new labor market entrants; %2-3 normal range, healthy labor mobility indicator
• Structural Unemployment: Technology-induced skill obsolescence, geographic mismatches, industrial decline; requires retraining/relocation; automation impact significant
• Cyclical Unemployment: Business cycle fluctuations result; recession → layoffs, expansion → hiring; most volatile component, policy responsive
• Natural Rate (NAIRU): Non-Accelerating Inflation Rate of Unemployment; frictional + structural unemployment; full employment seviyesi; %4-6 developed countries
• Phillips Curve Trade-off: Short-term unemployment-inflation inverse relationship; long-term expectations matter; stagflation (1970s) challenged traditional view

**Örnek:** 👥 2025'te ABD işsizlik oranı %3.8'de kaldı; teknoloji sektöründe beceri açığı %25 arttı.

---

## 12. Para Politikası ve Merkez Bankası

• Interest Rate Policy: Federal funds rate (policy rate) → transmission mechanism → market rates; contractionary (higher rates) vs. expansionary (lower rates) stance
• Money Supply Control: Quantitative easing (QE) → bond purchases → money injection; quantitative tightening (QT) → balance sheet reduction; M0, M1, M2 monetary aggregates
• Inflation Targeting Framework: Price stability mandate (2% target most countries); dual mandate (Fed): price stability + full employment; credibility crucial for expectations
• Policy Tools: Open market operations (primary), reserve requirements, discount window lending, forward guidance, unconventional tools (QE, negative rates)
• Transmission Channels: Interest rate channel, credit channel, exchange rate channel, wealth effect, expectations channel → real economy impact

**Örnek:** 🏦 2025'te TCMB faizi %8.5'e düşürdü; kredi kullanımı %15 arttı, ekonomik büyüme hızlandı.

---

## 13. Maliye Politikası ve Devlet Bütçesi

• Government Spending Policy: Infrastructure investment (multiplier effect), social programs, defense spending → aggregate demand stimulus; Keynesian fiscal multiplier theory
• Taxation Policy: Tax cuts → disposable income ↑ → consumption ↑; progressive vs. regressive tax systems; Laffer curve (tax rates vs. revenue relationship)
• Budget Balance Dynamics: Deficit spending → economic stimulus (short-term) vs. debt sustainability (long-term); debt-to-GDP ratio critical threshold (~90%)
• Automatic Stabilizers: Progressive income tax, unemployment insurance → counter-cyclical effects without legislative action; built-in recession buffers
• Fiscal Policy Challenges: Crowding out effect (government borrowing → higher interest rates), time lags, political constraints, debt sustainability concerns

**Örnek:** 💼 2025'te ABD'nin 1.7 trilyon dolarlık altyapı yatırımı istihdamı %2.5 artırdı.

---

## 14. Uluslararası Ticaret ve Küreselleşme

• Comparative Advantage Theory (David Ricardo): Countries specialize in production where they have lowest opportunity cost; mutual gains from trade even if one country more efficient in everything
• Free Trade Benefits: Consumer surplus ↑ (lower prices, variety), producer surplus ↑ (export markets), economic efficiency (resource allocation), innovation competition
• Trade Protection Tools: Tariffs (tax on imports), quotas (quantity limits), subsidies, non-tariff barriers; short-term industry protection vs. long-term economic cost
• Globalization Dynamics: Economic integration (trade, investment), technology diffusion, labor mobility, cultural exchange; benefits vs. inequality concerns
• Modern Trade Issues: Trade wars impact, supply chain resilience, digital trade rules, environmental standards, labor standards in trade agreements

**Örnek:** 🌍 2025'te CPTPP anlaşması üye ülkelerde ticaret hacmini %18 artırdı; teknoloji transferi hızlandı.

---

## 15. Davranışsal Ekonomi

• Behavioral Economics Foundation: Psychology meets economics; challenges rational actor assumption; bounded rationality (Herbert Simon), systematic cognitive biases affect decisions
• Major Cognitive Biases: Anchoring (first information influence), overconfidence (ability overestimation), loss aversion (losses hurt more than gains), confirmation bias
• Nudge Theory (Thaler/Sunstein): Choice architecture design → behavior influence without restricting options; libertarian paternalism concept
• Policy Applications: Auto-enrollment pension plans, opt-out vs opt-in organ donation, tax compliance simplification, energy conservation programs
• Market Implications: Predictable irrationality creates market opportunities; behavioral finance applications; consumer choice modeling for businesses

**Örnek:** 🧠 2025'te UK'da emeklilik otomatik katılımı %85'e çıktı; nudge politikalarıyla tasarruf arttı.

---

## 16. Çevre Ekonomisi ve Sürdürülebilirlik

• Environmental Externalities: Market failures where pollution costs not reflected in prices; negative externalities (cost to society) vs. positive externalities (benefits)
• Carbon Pricing Mechanisms: Carbon tax (direct price on emissions), cap-and-trade systems (emission permits), border carbon adjustments; internalize environmental costs
• Green Economy Transition: Renewable energy investments, circular economy principles, clean technology innovation, sustainable supply chains, green jobs creation
• Sustainable Development Goals: Brundtland Commission definition; triple bottom line (people, planet, profit); intergenerational equity principle
• Policy Instruments: Environmental regulations, green bonds, ecological tax reform, payment for ecosystem services, natural capital accounting

**Örnek:** 🌱 2025'te AB'nin yeşil anlaşması karbon emisyonlarını %30 azalttı; temiz enerji yatırımları 500 milyar €'ya ulaştı.

---

## 17. Kripto Para ve Dijital Ekonomi

• Blockchain Technology: Distributed ledger technology (DLT), cryptographic security, consensus mechanisms (Proof of Work, Proof of Stake); eliminates trusted intermediaries
• Digital Assets Classification: Cryptocurrencies (Bitcoin - store of value), utility tokens (Ethereum - smart contracts), stablecoins (price stability), CBDCs (central bank digital currencies)
• DeFi Ecosystem: Decentralized exchanges (DEXs), lending protocols, yield farming, automated market makers (AMMs); traditional finance disruption potential
• Macroeconomic Implications: Monetary policy transmission effects, financial stability risks, payment system innovation, monetary sovereignty challenges
• Regulatory Challenges: Consumer protection, anti-money laundering (AML), taxation framework, international coordination, innovation vs. stability balance

**Örnek:** ₿ 2025'te El Salvador'un Bitcoin rezervleri %40 değer kazandı; dijital ekonomi GDP'nin %15'ine ulaştı.

---

## 18. Ekonomik Krizler ve Toparlanma

• Crisis Typology: Financial market crashes (dot-com, 2008), banking crises (credit crunches), currency crises (balance of payments), sovereign debt crises
• Crisis Anatomy: Excessive leverage, asset bubbles formation, external shocks (oil crises), confidence collapses, contagion effects across markets/countries
• Recovery Strategies: Coordinated fiscal-monetary response, bank recapitalization, structural reforms (labor markets, regulatory), international assistance (IMF)
• Crisis Prevention: Early warning indicators (debt/GDP, current account deficits), macro-prudential tools (capital buffers, stress tests), regulatory frameworks
• Historical Lessons: Great Depression → New Deal policies, 2008 → Basel III regulations, COVID-19 → massive stimulus packages, supply chain resilience focus

**Örnek:** 📉 2025'te Türkiye ekonomisi %5.2 büyüdü; 2018 krizinden tamamen toparlandı (TCMB verileri).

---

## 19. Ekonominin Geleceği ve Yeni Trendler

• AI-Driven Economy: Machine learning automation, productivity surge, job displacement vs. creation debate; need for reskilling programs, universal basic income discussions
• Multipolar Economic Order: US-China tech rivalry, EU regulatory leadership, emerging markets (India, Brazil) rising; trade bloc formation, supply chain regionalization
• Circular Economy Transition: Waste-to-resource model, product life extension, sharing economy platforms; biomimicry innovation, regenerative business models
• Future of Work: Remote-first companies, gig economy expansion, digital nomad visas; work-life integration, metaverse workplaces, 4-day work week experiments
• Emerging Challenges: Aging populations (healthcare costs), climate adaptation investments, space economy development, quantum computing economic impact

**Örnek:** 🚀 2025'te ChatGPT ve AI araçları küresel üretkenliği %12 artırdı; 50 milyon yeni dijital iş oluştu.
